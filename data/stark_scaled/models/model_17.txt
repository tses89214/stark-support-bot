## Stark 17
- **Description**: A deep-space survey vessel built to map asteroid fields and gather mineral samples.  
- **Size**: 42 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 64 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 5 passengers.  
- **Color**: Slate Teal.  
- **Price**: $8,600,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Sample Core Drill, Lidar Mapping Mast.  
- **Notes**: Calibrate the lidar mast every 200 flight hours.
