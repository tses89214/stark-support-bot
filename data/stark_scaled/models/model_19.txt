## Stark 19
- **Description**: A courier craft optimised for same-day parcel runs between neighbouring stations.  
- **Size**: 12 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 14 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 1 passenger and up to 2 metric tons of parcels.  
- **Color**: Signal Yellow.  
- **Price**: $1,650,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Parcel Locker Rack, Express Docking Clamp.  
- **Notes**: Keep the parcel locker rack latched during acceleration.
