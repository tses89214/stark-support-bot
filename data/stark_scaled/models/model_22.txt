## Stark 22
- **Description**: A firefighting ship that douses station and depot fires with pressurised coolant.  
- **Size**: 36 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 62 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 6 passengers.  
- **Color**: Fire Red.  
- **Price**: $8,900,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Coolant Water Cannon, Smoke Filtration Mask Set.  
- **Notes**: Refill the coolant tanks only with the approved blue fluid.
