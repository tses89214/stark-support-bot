## Stark 12
- **Description**: A heavy-duty cargo hauler for moving freight between orbital stations.  
- **Size**: 80 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 400 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 3 crew, 250 tons of cargo.  
- **Color**: Industrial Gray.  
- **Price**: $12,000,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Cargo Crane Arm, Magnetic Container Locks.  
- **Notes**: Not licensed for passengers. Cargo must be balanced within 5% across both holds before takeoff.
