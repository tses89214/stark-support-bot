## Stark 15 Owner's Manual
- **Steering**: Stark 15 uses a force-feedback yoke. Pull to climb, push to dive. Feedback strength is set from 1 to 5 in the Controls menu; use 2 or lower with gloves.
- **Brakes**: Stark 15 stops with retro-thruster braking. A full stop from cruise speed takes up to 40 seconds. The brake pedal blends thrusters and drag fins automatically.
- **Throttle**: Stark 15 has a foot-pedal throttle with cruise hold. Press the cruise button at the speed you want and release the pedal; any brake input cancels cruise hold.
- **Climate**: Stark 15 has dual-zone climate control from 16 to 28 degrees Celsius with a separate cockpit and cabin setting. The filter must be replaced every 4 months.
- **Interior**: Stark 15 has seating for 5 passengers; it has bench seating covered in washable vinyl. The covers can be hosed down with fresh water.
- **Service interval**: Service Stark 15 every 175 flight hours. Serial numbers start with SK15.
- **Warning**: Never fly with the cabin hatch seal warning lit.
