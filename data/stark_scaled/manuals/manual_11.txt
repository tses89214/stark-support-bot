## Stark 11 Owner's Manual
- **Steering**: Stark 11 uses a fly-by-wire steering wheel. Turning the wheel yaws the ship; the wheel has a 540-degree lock-to-lock range and self-centres within 3 seconds of release.
- **Brakes**: Stark 11 stops with retro-thruster braking. A full stop from cruise speed takes up to 40 seconds. The brake pedal blends thrusters and drag fins automatically.
- **Throttle**: Stark 11 has a slider throttle with 5 detents. Detent 3 is cruise. Moving the slider past detent 4 needs the safety latch to be released.
- **Climate**: Stark 11 has a fan-only cabin system with no active cooling. In hot docks, open the vent shutters before boarding. The filter must be replaced every 3 months.
- **Interior**: Stark 11 has seating for 6 passengers; it has bench seating covered in washable vinyl. The covers can be hosed down with fresh water.
- **Service interval**: Service Stark 11 every 175 flight hours. Serial numbers start with SK11.
- **Warning**: Never charge the power cell unattended overnight.
