## Stark 10 Owner's Manual
- **Steering**: Stark 10 uses a dual-joystick station. The left stick controls translation and the right stick controls rotation. Press both stick buttons for 2 seconds to centre the ship.
- **Brakes**: Stark 10 stops with retro-thruster braking. A full stop from cruise speed takes up to 40 seconds. The brake pedal blends thrusters and drag fins automatically.
- **Throttle**: Stark 10 has a foot-pedal throttle with cruise hold. Press the cruise button at the speed you want and release the pedal; any brake input cancels cruise hold.
- **Climate**: Stark 10 has a fan-only cabin system with no active cooling. In hot docks, open the vent shutters before boarding. The filter must be replaced every 3 months.
- **Interior**: Stark 10 has seating for 20 passengers; it has leather seats with wood-grain trim. Condition the leather every 3 months and keep it out of direct sunlight at the dock.
- **Service interval**: Service Stark 10 every 150 flight hours. Serial numbers start with SK10.
- **Warning**: Never leave the throttle unattended during docking.
