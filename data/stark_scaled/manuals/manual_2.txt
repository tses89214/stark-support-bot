## Stark 2 Owner's Manual
- **Steering**: Stark 2 uses a fly-by-wire steering wheel. Turning the wheel yaws the ship; the wheel has a 540-degree lock-to-lock range and self-centres within 3 seconds of release.
- **Brakes**: Stark 2 stops with a 3-stage magnetic retarder. Stage 1 is gentle, stage 3 is for emergencies. Do not use stage 3 below 200 metres of altitude because it overheats the coils.
- **Throttle**: Stark 2 has a slider throttle with 5 detents. Detent 3 is cruise. Moving the slider past detent 4 needs the safety latch to be released.
- **Climate**: Stark 2 has dual-zone climate control from 16 to 28 degrees Celsius with a separate cockpit and cabin setting. The filter must be replaced every 4 months.
- **Interior**: Stark 2 has seating for 5 passengers; it has fabric seats with an LED cabin strip. Clean with a damp cloth only; solvents fade the fabric.
- **Service interval**: Service Stark 2 every 150 flight hours. Serial numbers start with SK02.
- **Warning**: Never charge the power cell unattended overnight.
