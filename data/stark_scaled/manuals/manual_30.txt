## Stark 30 Owner's Manual
- **Steering**: Stark 30 uses a force-feedback yoke. Pull to climb, push to dive. Feedback strength is set from 1 to 5 in the Controls menu; use 2 or lower with gloves.
- **Brakes**: Stark 30 stops with a 3-stage magnetic retarder. Stage 1 is gentle, stage 3 is for emergencies. Do not use stage 3 below 200 metres of altitude because it overheats the coils.
- **Throttle**: Stark 30 has a foot-pedal throttle with cruise hold. Press the cruise button at the speed you want and release the pedal; any brake input cancels cruise hold.
- **Climate**: Stark 30 has single-zone climate control from 18 to 26 degrees Celsius. The filter must be replaced every 6 months.
- **Interior**: Stark 30 has seating for 14 passengers; it has bench seating covered in washable vinyl. The covers can be hosed down with fresh water.
- **Service interval**: Service Stark 30 every 150 flight hours. Serial numbers start with SK30.
- **Warning**: Never fly with the cabin hatch seal warning lit.
