## Stark 4 Owner's Manual
- **Steering**: Stark 4 uses a dual-joystick station. The left stick controls translation and the right stick controls rotation. Press both stick buttons for 2 seconds to centre the ship.
- **Brakes**: Stark 4 stops with retro-thruster braking. A full stop from cruise speed takes up to 40 seconds. The brake pedal blends thrusters and drag fins automatically.
- **Throttle**: Stark 4 has a slider throttle with 5 detents. Detent 3 is cruise. Moving the slider past detent 4 needs the safety latch to be released.
- **Climate**: Stark 4 has a fan-only cabin system with no active cooling. In hot docks, open the vent shutters before boarding. The filter must be replaced every 3 months.
- **Interior**: Stark 4 has seating for 12 passengers; it has fabric seats with an LED cabin strip. Clean with a damp cloth only; solvents fade the fabric.
- **Service interval**: Service Stark 4 every 100 flight hours. Serial numbers start with SK04.
- **Warning**: Never leave the throttle unattended during docking.
