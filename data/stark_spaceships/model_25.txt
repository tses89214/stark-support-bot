## Stark 25
- **Description**: A bulk tanker for moving water ice and fuel between depots.  
- **Size**: 80 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 210 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 3 passengers and up to 180 metric tons of liquid.  
- **Color**: Tank Green.  
- **Price**: $15,800,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Pump Manifold, Leak Detection Sensors.  
- **Notes**: Inspect the pump manifold gaskets every 90 flight hours.
