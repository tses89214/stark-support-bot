## Stark 16
- **Description**: A rapid-response medical ship with an onboard surgical bay for emergencies between stations.  
- **Size**: 38 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 55 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 6 passengers and 2 stretchers.  
- **Color**: Rescue Orange.  
- **Price**: $7,800,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Surgical Bay Module, Emergency Beacon Array.  
- **Notes**: Sterilise the surgical bay with the UV cycle after every flight.
