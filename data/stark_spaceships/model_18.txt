## Stark 18
- **Description**: A heavy mining barge for hauling ore from belt claims to the orbital yards.  
- **Size**: 70 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 150 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 4 passengers and up to 120 metric tons of ore.  
- **Color**: Rust Brown.  
- **Price**: $12,400,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Ore Crusher Hopper, Plasma Cutter Arm.  
- **Notes**: Rinse ore dust from the intake grilles after every haul.
