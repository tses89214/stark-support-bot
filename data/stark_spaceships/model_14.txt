## Stark 14
- **Description**: A research vessel equipped with laboratories for deep-space science missions.  
- **Size**: 55 meters in length.  
- **Weight**: 90 metric tons.  
- **Capacity**: 8 researchers.  
- **Color**: Teal Green.  
- **Price**: $15,000,000.  
- **Compatible Accessories**: Sample Containment Chamber, Telescope Array, Radiation Shield Upgrade.  
- **Notes**: Lab modules require a clean-room reset before each new mission to avoid sample contamination.
