## Ordering and Delivery Guide
- **How to order**: 1) Pick a model and accessories. 2) Request a quote from customer_service@stack_spaceship.com. 3) Pay a 20% deposit to confirm the order. 4) Pay the balance before delivery.
- **Delivery time**: Standard models ship in 6 to 8 weeks after the deposit. Custom colors or accessory bundles add 2 weeks.
- **On arrival**: 1) Inspect the hull, thrusters and canopy with the delivery agent present. 2) Sign the delivery form only after the inspection. 3) Report any damage within 7 days.
- **Damage or late delivery**: Contact customer service with your order number. A human agent opens a ticket and replies within 2 business days.
- **Returns**: Unused ships can be returned within 14 days of delivery; the deposit is refunded minus a 5% handling fee.
